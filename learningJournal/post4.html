Post 4 - Game Design Research

Post 4 • Game Design Research

What I Learned

I read “I was asked to invent the next Wordle. How hard could it be?” and it honestly made me realize how much work goes into something that seems really simple at first. Wordle looks like a basic guessing game, but the article shows there is a lot more thinking behind it than I expected.

One thing that stood out to me was how careful you have to be with the word list. It is not just picking random words, it has to feel fair and not annoying. If the words are too common, the game gets boring, but if they are too rare or weird, people just get frustrated and quit. I also did not really think about how much balance matters in games like this. The difficulty has to feel just right so players feel challenged but not stuck all the time.

Another thing I found interesting was how small design choices change the whole experience. Even things like how feedback is shown or how the game reacts when you guess wrong can affect how people feel while playing. It made me realize games are not just about rules, they are also about the experience and emotions behind them.
