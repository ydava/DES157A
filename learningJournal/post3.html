What I Learned

From reading I lerned that images arent just there to look pretty or decorate sometimes images tell a story. Visual thinking is about slowing down and REALLY look at an image.The article on “intriguing photographs” shows how small details in a picture can change your understanding if you take time to notice them. It made me realize I usually rush past images without thinking deeply about what is actually happening in them or just look at them at face value.

10 Intriguing Photographs

I also explored Awwwards.com, which shows creative websites that use images and interaction in interesting ways. Some sites are really engaging because you can scroll, click, or move through the design, which makes the experience more fun and memorable. The strength of these websites is that they grab your attention and make you explore the visuals more carefully. However, a weakness is that some designs are confusing or too busy, which can make it harder to understand the main idea.

Overall, I think visual thinking is about paying attention and questioning what you see. Websites like the ones on Awwwards help with that, but only when the design is clear enough to guide you instead of overwhelming you.
